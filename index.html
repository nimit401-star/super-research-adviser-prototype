Important distinction: APRA does not calculate the Research Match Score shown above. APRA publishes the underlying product data and the legislated performance-test result. This prototype applies its own disclosed weighting and peer-ranking rules to create a research starting point.

Fees and returns are ranked against products with the required data for the selected balance and period. For balances between APRA's published points, annual fee dollars are estimated between the two closest points. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance. APRA data does not cover every suitability factor, including insurance, eligibility, features and service quality.
